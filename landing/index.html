Who it's for

Robot fleet operators, and the people booking them for one-off jobs.

The problem

Robots are expensive to own and hard to book, and quoting a job by hand is slow and error-prone.

Quickstart

Python 3.10+, no other dependencies. Your release archive includes the wheel and examples.

pip install robot_rental-0.1.0-py3-none-any.whl
robot-rental fleet
robot-rental quote move --rooms 3 --heaviest 80 --stairs --date 2026-11-07 --km 12
robot-rental quote event --guests 120 --hours 5 --date 2026-11-07
